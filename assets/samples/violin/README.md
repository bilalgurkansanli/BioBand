# Violin samples

Bowed (arco) single-note MP3s for the violin engine (nearest-anchor sampler).

Source: [nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) `samples/violin/`  
Also published as [`tonejs-instrument-violin-mp3`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/tonejs-instrument-violin-mp3).  
Public-domain / CC-by sample pack (see upstream `sample-source-info.txt`).

| Anchors | MIDI |
|---------|------|
| G3, A3, C4, E4, G4, A4, C5, E5, G5, A5, C6, E6 (12 notes) | 55–88 |

Covers open G3 through high positions on E (~C#6).

## Note

Older `v1_E5.mp3`…`v4_G3.mp3` placeholders (~0.35 s synthetic blips) were replaced — they did not sound like a real violin.
